The Origin Thread

A guided exercise to surface the through-line that's been running quietly underneath your work, your relationships, the chapters of your life that seem unrelated until you look again.

You'll move through a sequence of prompts — memories, images, recurring symbols, the things you've always come back to — and end with one written sentence: the thread that's been there all along.

Most people use it as a foundation for everything else: brand language, a body of work, a creative project, or simply a more honest sense of what they're here to do.

Format · Notion template Time · 15–30 min Cost · Free
