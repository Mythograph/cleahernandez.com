How It Works

Signal, not slogans.

We start with what wants to be witnessed. We listen for what's true, what's repeated, what's avoided, and what's trying to emerge.

We excavate story and psyche. This is where the real differentiators and emotional fidelity live.

We architect the system. We translate what we find into structures you can use: frameworks, maps, language sets, and decision rules.

We apply it in real contexts. Live pages, key emails, offer messaging, or content — so the system becomes embodied, not theoretical.

This is collaborative, iterative work. You won't leave with "a doc." You'll leave with a narrative operating system you can confidently use and evolve on your own.

A typical flow (Story Loom = 6 weeks)

Coaching Sessions

Weekly 60-minute sessions focused on the "shadow" material that blocks clean visibility — Who do you think you are? authority discomfort, symbols, ancestral messages, the parts that don't want to be seen. I synthesize what emerges into a Golden Thread Report.

Creative Exercises

During and between sessions, simple prompts and practices (Active Imagination, dreamwork cues, narrative reflection) to keep the signal moving and surface language we can build from.

Async Support

Voxer/email Mon–Fri, responses within 24–48 business hours for questions, voice-note processing, and quick calibration between sessions.

Weeks 4–6: Build

We gather your signature stories, conviction points, and lived proof — then translate them into clear aligned messaging and apply the system to your real-world assets.
