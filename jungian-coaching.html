The Method
"Who looks outside, dreams;
who looks inside, awakes."

— Carl Jung

Jungian coaching is a reflective, depth-oriented conversation that helps you understand what's really happening beneath the surface of a problem. Carl Jung's insight was that the unconscious is not a dumping ground for what we don't want to know, but is instead a generative field of narrative material, symbolic imagery, and unlived possibility.

Instead of focusing on goals and fixes, we pay attention to patterns that repeat, the feelings that arrive, and the images and metaphors your psyche uses to communicate — including dreams, if you bring them to a session.

The aim is not "self-improvement" or optimization. It's not driven by goals, which are constructs of our ego. It's individuation: tapping into what is beneath the surface to become more whole, more honest, and more aligned with the life that wants to live through you.

This is also not therapy, nor is it crisis support. It's a peer-to-peer relationship, where I ask lots of questions, invite you down paths, and notice what comes up, so you can more easily reflect and map your own inner landscape.

What it's for

This container supports you when you want:

  • More self-trust and autonomy in decision-making
  • Relief from repeating internal loops (without bypassing what they protect)
  • A deeper relationship with your own inner authority
  • Language for what you know but can't quite articulate yet
  • A way to metabolize a transition without rushing to "solve" it
  • Grounded next steps that match who you're becoming

How I practice

My approach is Jungian at its core, but I'm also trained in several adjacent modalities that I weave in when they serve the work. I treat psyche-language as real language, and I work with it directly.

Alongside dreamwork and shadow work, I use creative writing, active imagination, and other creative exercises to help you communicate with hidden parts of yourself and reframe outdated stories.

Depending on what serves you, sessions can draw from archetypal and somatic modalities, including:

  • Reiki
  • Human Design
  • EFT (tapping)
  • Western astrology
