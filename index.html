Instead, I found myself in a catch-22 as a strategist trying to build my own brand: I realized the strategic tools available to entrepreneurs were built for the game that's eating us alive. At best, the language doesn't quite fit. At worst, it degrades our integrity and trust, the communities we're here to serve, and the planet we all share.

Most entrepreneurs are still building on a scarcity story they inherited. If you're building something that matters — a practice, a body of work, a way of being in the world shaped by what you believe is possible — your work deserves a narrative field that's genuine and expansive.

Right now, we need stories from people with vision who aren't selling the status quo.

You need to tell your stories, and we need to hear them.

This is what I do.

I work at the intersection of brand strategy and Jungian depth psychology to help you notice what's actually shaping your story beneath the surface: the beliefs you inherited, the roles you learned to play, the parts of you that don't feel safe being seen. Once you've started interpreting the messages your body is telling you, you can begin to reframe your story using intentional storytelling. You stop forcing the persona that kept you safe before, because you no longer need it. Then we turn that material into honest, resonant language, you start speaking and writing confidently from inner authority, and your brand becomes a coherent narrative people can feel and trust.

How It Works

Signal over slogans.

We start with what wants to be witnessed. We listen for what's true, what's repeated, what's avoided, and what's trying to emerge.

We excavate story and psyche. This is where the real differentiators and emotional fidelity live.

We architect the system. We translate what we find into structures you can use: frameworks, maps, language sets, and decision rules.

We apply it in real contexts. Live pages, key emails, offer messaging, or content — so the system becomes embodied, not theoretical.

This is collaborative, iterative work. You won't leave with "a doc." You'll leave with a narrative operating system you can confidently use and evolve on your own.

A typical flow

Jungian Coaching Sessions

Weekly 60-minute sessions focused on finding the narrative material that feels most alive. We work with whatever blocks clean visibility — the voice that asks Who do you think you are? The shadow archetypes, discomfort, symbols, ancestral messages, and all the parts that don't want to be seen. I synthesize what emerges into a Golden Thread Report so we have an emotional/narrative anchor before we build anything.

Creative Exercises

During and between sessions, you'll get simple prompts and practices (e.g., Active Imagination meditations, dreamwork cues, narrative reflection, tapping exercises) to keep the signal moving and to surface language we can build from.

Async Support (Voxer/email)

Mon–Fri, responses within 24–48 business hours for questions, voice-note processing, story recollections, and quick calibration between sessions.

Weeks 4–6 (consulting + build)

We gather your signature stories, conviction points, and lived proof, translate them into clear aligned messaging, and apply the system to your real-world assets.

Weeks 6–8 (Full Build only)

We translate the narrative system into a live site — visual direction, page structure, and drafted copy turned into a published 3–5 page website.
